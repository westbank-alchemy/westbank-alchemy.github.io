# About

I am a political scientist and applied researcher focused on media and information environments, public opinion, foreign influence, and international development. My research examines how media affect democratization and how development finance shapes public attitudes.

I most recently worked as a Research Scientist at AidData at William & Mary, where I designed and led cross-national research using quantitative and qualitative methods, with a particular focus on Chinese and Russian foreign influence. I also served for two years as a scientist with the International Panel on the Information Environment (IPIE). My work includes survey analysis, statistical modeling, index construction, and communicating research findings to academic and policy audiences.

Before becoming a researcher, I worked as an outreach coordinator for the City of New Orleans from 2009 to 2013, supporting community development projects in the years following Hurricane Katrina.

This website brings together my academic publications, replication materials, and selected applied research.

I also write about travel and history on my Substack, Some Odd Road, exploring the intersection of memory, culture, geopolitics, local life, all seen through my personal experience. Always ready to talk travel, especially Latin America and the Balkans. Forever a Louisianan.
