Data

Media Resilience to Malign Influence (MRMI) Index, Version 1.0 (2022)

An index measuring media resilience to foreign malign influence across 17 countries in Europe and Eurasia from 2010 to 2020. Developed in collaboration with USAID.

[CSV] [Stata] [Excel & Codebook] [README] [Report]

Data licensed under ODC-By 1.0.

Media Freedom Estimates (MSFS) (2020)

Media freedom estimates developed by Solis and Waggoner using item response theory, as presented in "Measuring Media Freedom: An Item Response Theory Analysis of Existing Indicators," published in the British Journal of Political Science.
